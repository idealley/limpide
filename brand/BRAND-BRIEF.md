# Limpide — Brand Brief

*Version 0.1 — June 2026. The reference for the wordmark, the landing page, and any future surface. This brief governs how Limpide looks and sounds in public; it does not describe the pedagogy (that lives in `docs/`). The public face is deliberately quieter than the design docs.*

---

## 1. The essence, in one line

> **Limpide helps a mind become clear.**

Not *learn faster*. Not *score higher*. Clear — the way water is clear. The name carries the whole brand: *limpide* (French) means transparent, untroubled, easy to see through. It describes the state a person is in when they have genuinely understood something, and that state is the only thing we sell.

## 2. What we lead with

Two promises, held together, addressed to two audiences who want the same thing:

- **Understanding over memorization.** Knowledge is now a commodity — it's in every pocket, free and instant. What stays scarce, and what now matters, is the ability to *understand* what you learn: to rebuild it, connect it, and know it's yours. Limpide is for the understanding, not the facts.
- **Becoming a clear thinker.** The outcome a parent wants for their child, and an adult wants for themselves: to reason well, to read deeply, and to say what you mean. A clear mind, built on purpose.

Both audiences are first-class. Copy should say *your child — or you*, never assume only K-12. The parent buying for a nine-year-old and the adult rebuilding their own thinking are the same customer with the same wish.

## 3. What we deliberately do **not** say

The method is invisible on every public surface. This is a brand rule, not a preference.

- **No "Socratic," no "tutor-bot," no "teach-back," no "AI tutor."** The mechanism is genuinely interesting, but naming it cheapens it and invites comparison to a crowded category. We sell the result and the feeling; the *how* is discovered by using it.
- **No edtech clichés.** No "supercharge," "unlock your potential," "level up," "10x," "gamified," "personalized learning journey," badges, streaks, or leaderboards. These signal the exact thing we are not.
- **No hype punctuation.** Effectively no exclamation marks. Confidence is shown by calm, not volume.
- **No fear-selling to parents.** We do not trade on anxiety ("don't let your child fall behind"). We trade on aspiration and respect for the learner.
- **No overclaiming.** No "guaranteed results," no test-score promises. The brand's credibility is its restraint.

## 4. Personality

If Limpide were a person: a calm, well-read teacher with quiet authority. Serious without being severe. Warm without being soft. Modern, but rooted in something older than the latest app. Comfortable with silence. Never in a hurry, never selling.

Five adjectives, in priority order: **clear, calm, serious, warm, quietly modern.**

The "subtle tech" the founder asked for lives here — not in neon or gradients, but in precision: a fine hairline, a small monospaced label, generous whitespace, exact alignment. The feeling of an instrument that was built carefully, sitting on a paper desk. Craft, not gadgetry.

## 5. Voice

Short sentences. Plain words used precisely. Literary restraint — closer to a good essay than a sales page. We can be quietly bold ("Knowledge is everywhere now. Understanding is rare."), but we never shout and never wink.

Voice tests — would Limpide say it?
- ✅ "Understanding that stays with you long after the lesson is over."
- ✅ "Le savoir est partout. Comprendre est rare."
- ❌ "Master any subject 10x faster with AI!"
- ❌ "Your personalized Socratic learning journey starts now 🚀"

Bilingual note: French is not a translation of English, nor the reverse. Each is written natively. French leans on the *apprendre / comprendre* distinction that English has lost — use it; it is a brand asset, not a tagline to translate around. Inclusive forms (*inscrit·e*, *informé·e*) are used in French.

## 6. Naming and taglines

**Wordmark:** *Limpide* — lowercase or capitalized "L", always in the script face (see §7). Never set in the body font.

**Primary tagline**
- EN: **Learn to understand.**
- FR: **Apprendre à comprendre.**

**The supporting line** (hero subhead, sets the thesis without the method)
- EN: *Knowledge is everywhere now. Understanding is rare.*
- FR: *Le savoir est partout. Comprendre est rare.*

**Alternates** (for other surfaces / A-B testing; keep one voice)
- EN: "Understanding that lasts." · "A clearer mind." · "Where understanding takes root." · "Built to be understood."
- FR: "Une pensée claire." · "Comprendre, vraiment." · "La clarté s'apprend." · "Ce qui reste, une fois la leçon finie."

**Three quiet pillars** (optional, used as a calm triad, never as feature bullets)
- EN: Understand deeply · Think clearly · Express yourself
- FR: Comprendre en profondeur · Penser clairement · S'exprimer

## 7. Visual identity

### Wordmark
A cursive / script "Limpide" — flowing, elegant, legible, serious rather than ornamental or bridal. It is the single most important brand asset and usually appears alone, large, with air around it.

Recommended faces (Google Fonts, swap-friendly via one CSS variable):
1. **Pinyon Script** — classic copperplate, elegant and clear (current default).
2. **Petit Formal Script** — slightly more substantial, formal.
3. **Sacramento** — lighter, calmer, more modern if Pinyon feels too formal.
4. Fallback for a less literal "cursive": **Cormorant Garamond**, italic — refined, not a true script.

Try the wordmark in all four before settling; the choice sets the entire mood. Pair it with restraint everywhere else.

### Typography system
- **Wordmark / brand moments:** the script face above.
- **Headings:** a literary serif — **Cormorant Garamond** (light/regular). Large, airy, low-contrast with the page.
- **Body:** a clean, quiet sans — **Inter** (or system UI sans). Comfortable, neutral, never trendy.
- **Micro-labels / tech accent:** a monospace — **IBM Plex Mono** or **JetBrains Mono**, small, letter-spaced, used sparingly for things like `PRIVATE BETA · 2026` and the `FR / EN` toggle. This is where "subtle tech" lives.

### Colour — "paper and ink"
| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Paper (background) | `#F4EFE5` | The dominant field. Warm, off-white, like good stock. |
| Paper, deeper | `#ECE4D5` | Panels, the form area, subtle separation. |
| Ink | `#26221E` | Primary text. Near-black, warm, never pure `#000`. |
| Ink, soft | `#5A5248` | Secondary text, captions. |
| Accent — petrol | `#2F4F4A` | Sparingly: button fill, links, the hairline. A calm, deep teal-green; the "tech" note without coldness. |
| Accent, light | `#7A9A93` | Hover / focus states, faint lines. |

One accent only. The brand is paper, ink, and a single considered colour. Avoid gradients except a barely-perceptible paper vignette.

### Texture and motifs
- A very subtle paper grain (low-opacity noise) is welcome; it must never be obvious.
- "Subtle tech" motif: a single fine hairline (1px, petrol at low opacity), an optional faint dotted baseline grid in one corner, exact optical alignment. Think *engraver's plate*, not *dashboard*.
- No illustration of robots, brains, lightbulbs, graduation caps, or chat bubbles. If imagery is ever used, it is restrained and human: a hand, a book, light on paper.

### Layout principles
Generous margins. One idea per screen. The wordmark breathes. The waitlist form is calm and central, not a popup. Nothing blinks, bounces, or demands. The page should feel like opening a well-made notebook.

## 8. Accessibility and trust
- Ink-on-paper contrast meets WCAG AA for body text; check the petrol accent on paper for any text use.
- The email capture is self-hosted (Cloudflare Worker + D1) — say so quietly. A one-line promise near the form — *"One email when we open. No noise, ever."* / *"Un seul email au lancement. Jamais de bruit."* — is part of the brand (restraint, respect, sovereignty over data).

## 9. Status
**Settled:** the essence, the two lead promises, the method-invisible rule, the voice, the paper-and-ink direction. **Tentative:** the exact script face for the wordmark (test the four), the precise accent hex, the primary tagline (vs. the alternates). **Open:** logo lockup beyond the wordmark, any motif system, domain and social handles.
