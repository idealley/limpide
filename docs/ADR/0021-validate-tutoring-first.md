# ADR-0021: Validate tutoring first; defer the network business

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-09-10

Amends ADR-0009, ADR-0012, ADR-0016, ADR-0018, Foundation rules F1 and F2, and the commercial sequencing in `STRATEGY.md`; clarifies ADR-0013. Earlier ADR text remains as history; this decision takes precedence where it conflicts.

## Context

A September 2026 review of the design documents found a credible proposition: help a student expose a missing prerequisite through explanation, offer a choice about exploring it, and remember unfinished work across sessions. Respecting urgent needs and making confusion safe to express are central strengths.

It also identified untested assumptions presented as settled: discovery always beats explicit teaching; process scores establish durable understanding and institutional efficacy; pedagogy, measurement, and sovereignty are already moats; individual, institutional, and network products form a proven commercial sequence.

The founder accepted these concerns and chose to defer the network to a future business goal. The founder has teacher contacts and proposes placement followed by a daily exercise as a possible first offer. That offer remains a hypothesis (`../BOOTSTRAP-PILOT.md`), not a launched service or a confirmed pilot arrangement.

## Decision

**Validate one useful tutoring experience first.** Start with a bounded subject and cohort, using teacher collaboration for content and assessment. Mathematics in Vaud remains the recommendation. Test learner benefit, voluntary return, teacher usefulness, willingness to pay, and delivery cost. A small pilot supplies directional evidence, not a general efficacy claim.

**Keep values firm and teaching methods testable.** Learner dignity, agency, honest feedback, privacy, and safeguarding remain commitments. Guided discovery, explanation, correction, worked examples, and practice are available teaching moves. F1 becomes a preference for helping students notice, with timely direct help when questioning is unproductive or the learner requests it. Withholding is not an end in itself.

**Separate teaching route from evidence of understanding.** Record hints, explanations, corrections, and independent attempts. F2 gives stronger evidential weight to independent demonstration than repetition of supplied content; it does not mandate an intrinsic bonus for a discovery teaching method. A student can demonstrate understanding after explicit instruction. Outcome weights, exam-mode discounts, and different decay rates are provisional hypotheses to calibrate, not empirical truths required by the philosophy.

**Validate process signals against independent outcomes.** Retain explanation trajectories, support attribution, and uncertainty. Add delayed, unaided explanation and unfamiliar application tasks using a fixed rubric with educator review. Validation must not simply repeat the model's own assumptions. Independent assessment complements process evidence. Distinguish observed progress from claims that Limpide or an institution caused it; causal claims require an appropriate comparison and study design.

**Validate the buyer separately.** Actionable formative information for teachers is the initial institutional hypothesis. Longitudinal efficacy reporting is a possible later offering after validity and demand are established. Socratic behavior and sovereign storage are product qualities; defensibility must be earned through useful delivery, content, distribution, relationships, and evidence.

**Defer network business without a calendar commitment.** Cross-institution benchmarks, contribution incentives, subscriptions, and isolation premiums are future options. Exclude them from the pilot offer, revenue assumptions, and required implementation. Revisit after tutoring works, institutions pay, measures are comparable, and eligible institutions want to participate. Preserve privacy boundaries and stable concept identifiers without requiring a complete network design before testing the product. No new data sharing is authorized; child-data restrictions remain in force.

**Seed the graph from official curriculum material.** Import the selected official curriculum before collecting learner data, as ADR-0013's import-and-overlay model already describes. Extraction and LLM-assisted decomposition draft concepts and prerequisites; teachers validate the imported and inferred structure rather than authoring it from scratch. Import coverage may be broader than the first validated tutoring slice.

**Treat daily practice as a proposed experiment.** Optional placement and recurring exercises could provide repeatable benefit and longitudinal observations. Placement uses school year/program (age as a rough prior), optional photos of recent teacher-assigned exercises, and a few targeted questions; checked exercises and explanations are generated from the selected objective and learner evidence. Encounters refine individual estimates and suggest reviewed changes; they do not automatically establish shared prerequisites. Mathematics comes first in the proposal; physics and philosophy need separate content, assessment, and demand tests.

## Alternatives considered

- **Build all commercial scales together:** deferred because demand and measurement validity remain untested.
- **Judge success only through internal scores:** rejected because weights and decay can encode the desired conclusion.
- **Preserve absolute Socratic withholding:** rejected because timely explanation can support subsequent independent demonstration.
- **Launch three subjects together:** deferred because it divides content and recruitment effort and obscures why an offer succeeds or fails.

## Consequences

The active strategy leads with learner and teacher value. The pilot includes independent assessment and commercial validation. Network launch dates and pricing commitments are removed. Foundation evaluations must permit explicit teaching while distinguishing supported responses from independent demonstrations.

The daily offer, cohort, cluster, price, cadence, placement design, and recruitment arrangements remain proposed. Amendment notes in ADRs 0009, 0012, 0013, 0016, and 0018 record this decision's effect on each.

## Evidence informing the review

- [Alfieri et al. (2011)](https://psycnet.apa.org/record/2010-23599-001): assisted and unassisted discovery have different outcomes; the findings support instructional flexibility, not universal discovery superiority.
- [Bastani et al. (2025)](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2422633122): assisted and subsequent unaided performance can diverge. This motivates external checks; it does not validate Limpide.
- [Khanmigo](https://www.khanmigo.ai/), checked 2026-09-10: guided questioning is already a competing product proposition. Limpide's advantage must be more specific.

## Status

**Settled.** Tutoring and buyer validation first; testable teaching mechanisms; independent evidence for efficacy claims; network business and pricing deferred; official curriculum material as the graph's initial source.

**Tentative.** Daily practice, placement design, candidate pilot size and duration, and mathematics-first recruitment.

**Open.** Teacher arrangements, learner age/curriculum, willingness to pay, assessment calibration, unit economics, and evidence needed for later subjects or commercial scales.
